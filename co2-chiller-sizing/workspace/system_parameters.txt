R744 transcritical CO2 chiller — vineyard wine storage design data
===================================================================

Evaporating temperature (Tev):       −2 °C
Useful superheat:                      5 K
Suction superheat:                    5 K
Gas-cooler exit temperature:         +35 °C
High-side pressure (gas cooler):     9.0 MPa
Refrigerant:                         R744 (CO2)
Compression efficiency (η_comp):     0.70
Volumetric efficiency (η_vol):       0.85
Design cooling load (Q_design):      18 kW

External boundaries (Carnot reference):
  Cellar air (T_L):                  13 °C
  Outdoor ambient (T_H):             32 °C

Notes:
  - Useful / suction superheat 5 K means the compressor inlet is
    5 K above saturation at evaporator pressure
    (compressor inlet temperature = +3 °C).
  - When useful SH = suction SH, evaporator outlet and compressor inlet coincide.
  - Heat rejection is via a gas cooler at controlled high-side pressure
    9.0 MPa — not a subcritical condenser.
  - Obtain R744 thermodynamic properties from any source you choose
    (tables, CoolProp/REFPROP, online tools, etc.) and document the source.
  - Deliverable must include cooling EER = q_evap / w_comp (W/W),
    Carnot comparison using T_L / T_H, mass flow ṁ = Q_design / q_evap,
    and displacement V̇_disp = ṁ · v1 / η_vol.
