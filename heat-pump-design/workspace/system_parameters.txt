Refrigerant:                       R1233zd(E)

Notes:

    saturation at evaporator pressure.
  - Suction superheat 6 K means the compressor inlet is 6 K above
    saturation at evaporator pressure (compressor inlet temperature = +41 °C).
  - When useful SH = suction SH, evaporator outlet and compressor inlet coincide.
  - Obtain R1233zd(E) thermodynamic properties from any source you choose
    (tables, CoolProp/REFPROP, online tools, etc.) and document the source.
  - Deliverable must include heating COP in W/W: COP = q_cond / w_comp.
    Cooling EER is not required. Compare to reverse Carnot COP using T_L and T_H
    (external water temperatures), not Tev/Tcond.
