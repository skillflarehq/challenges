Kipeta health-post minigrid — design data
==========================================

Site (synthetic): Kipeta, Dodoma hinterland, Tanzania
GPS: -6.82000, 36.48000
Do not treat this as a live project.

Peak-sun hours (PSH):              5.0 h/day
Performance ratio (PR):            0.72
Distribution + contingency loss:   0.20 (apply to served energy)
Days of autonomy:                  2.0
Battery depth of discharge (DoD):  0.80
Round-trip efficiency (η_rt):      0.90
Array packing (m2_per_kwp):        10 m²/kWp (modules + row spacing)

Plot rule:
  Choose the generation plot with excluded = false
  and area_m2 >= Array_area_m2.
  Use the plots.geojson attribute table (do not planimeter).
  Exactly one plot qualifies.

Formulas (use these; do not replace with HOMER / PVGIS):
  E_served      = sum of daily_kwh in loads.csv
  E_day         = E_served × (1 + loss_frac)
  PV_kWp        = E_day / (PSH × PR)
  Battery_kWh   = (E_day × autonomy_days) / (DoD × η_rt)
  Array_area_m2 = PV_kWp × m2_per_kwp
